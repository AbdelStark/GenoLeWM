  freezes Carbon-500M, encodes a reference window once to a pooled state

  effect of an edit action $a$ directly in latent space. Its entire value proposition is that

  \caption{\textbf{The sensitivity ceiling: a single-base edit is nearly invisible to cosine at
  every pooling width, and essentially invisible at global pooling.} Left axis (blue, solid):
  mean relative displacement $\lVert\Delta\rVert_2/\lVert s_{\mathrm{ref}}\rVert_2$. Right axis
  (red, dashed): mean $\cos(s_{\mathrm{ref}},s_{\mathrm{alt}})$. Both are means over the
  $14{,}000$-variant cohort. Sensitivity peaks at the narrowest pool ($r{=}8$, $\pm48$\,bp) and

  are cosine-identical to four decimal places ($0.99989$). \emph{This is not information loss.}

  (\cref{fig:auroc}). A world model trained to predict $s_{\mathrm{alt}}$ from
  $s_{\mathrm{ref}}$ under a cosine or absolute-state objective is therefore optimising against a
  target that ``predict-no-change'' already attains to within $10^{-4}$: the objective is
  degenerate because the metric is wrong, not because the signal is absent.}
  \label{fig:sensitivity}
\end{figure}}
